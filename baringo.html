About the Mission

The Baringo Mega Mission was a powerful outreach initiative aimed at impacting the community through spiritual empowerment, social support, and leadership engagement. The mission involved different ministries working together to spread the Gospel and serve the community through evangelism, teaching, and fellowship.

Activities That Took Place

  • Morning Devotions
  • Door to Door Evangelism
  • Crusades
  • Revivals
  • Sunday School Ministry
  • High School Ministry
