About Us

Noretsoret is a student-led union at the University of Nairobi dedicated to empowering students, developing leadership skills, and spreading the word of God within and beyond the campus.

Mission

To disciple members,to reach the whole world with the word of God

Vision

To be ambassadors and witnesses of Christ sharing the light of salvation in and out of campus
